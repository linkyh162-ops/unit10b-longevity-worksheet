      B - Collocations

      1. exceptional longevity - living an unusually long life
      2. a lean diet / lifestyle - low-fat food and healthy habits
      3. relieve stress / pain - reduce worry or discomfort
      4. a special privilege - a rare advantage or gift
      5. follow / give guidelines - use or offer rules for living well
      6. forbid / discourage smoking / alcohol / red meat - not allow or try to stop unhealthy habits

      C - Sentence Frames - Sample Completions

      These are model answers. Yours may differ.

      longevity

      One key factor in longevity is strong social connections, because friends and family can relieve stress and give life purpose.

      guideline

      A useful guideline for a healthy life is to stay active every day. For example, people in Sardinia work hard on farms and walk a lot.

      industrious

      Being industrious can help people live longer. In the reading, Tonino shows this because he works hard with his cows and stays busy.

      forbid / discourage

      Some communities forbid smoking and alcohol. They also discourage eating a lot of red meat in order to protect health and live longer.

      lean

      A lean diet means eating food that is low in fat. In Okinawa, people eat local vegetables and stop when they are 80% full.

      ironically

      Ironically, the healthy Okinawan diet actually helped people live longer, even though it was born of hardship and poverty.

      relieve

      Spending time with friends can relieve stress. As a result, people may enjoy better health and greater longevity.

      ikigai / moai

      My ikigai is learning and helping my classmates. Having a moai (support group) is important because it gives emotional support and purpose.

      D - Explore and E - Write

      There is no single correct answer. Strong responses should include:

      • A clear claim (your main idea)
      • Specific evidence from the reading (name, place, number, or habit)
      • At least one logical connector (because, for example, as a result, in addition, however)
      • A personal connection or deeper thought (Connect / Push Deeper)

      Use the model paragraph from the lesson PowerPoint: Claim - Evidence - Explanation.
