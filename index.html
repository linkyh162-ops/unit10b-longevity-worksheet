Word Bank

Tap each card to reveal meaning and example.

longevity noun
a long life; living for many years
...regions where exceptional longevity is the norm
guideline noun
a general rule or piece of advice
...three sets of guidelines to follow
industrious adjective
hardworking; always busy doing useful work
the industrious Tonino has already milked four cows...
barely adverb
only just; almost not
her head is barely higher than the steering wheel
privilege noun
a special advantage or opportunity
...the privilege of living so long
forbid verb
to officially not allow something
It forbids smoking, alcohol consumption...
discourage verb
to try to stop someone from doing something
The church also discourages the consumption of other meat...
lean (diet) adjective
low in fat; not heavy or rich
A lean diet of food grown on the island...
ironically adverb
in a way opposite to what you might expect
Ironically, this healthy way of eating was born of hardship.
relieve verb
to reduce or remove pain, stress, or a problem
...which helps to relieve stress
moai noun
a mutual support group of friends (Okinawa)
Many older Okinawans belong to a moai...
ikigai noun
what makes one's life worth living; life purpose
My ikigai is right here... (her friends)
